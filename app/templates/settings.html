Data policy

  • Snapshots are read-only; hashes are checked before and after analysis.
  • Drafts are written under data/runs/<run>/draft/ and never into the source.
  • The default path is fully offline: no telemetry, no tenant, no credentials.
  • Optional live reasoning is opt-in, env-configured, and limited to synthetic fixtures by default.
  • Secrets are redacted at capture; only a fingerprint is stored.
  • The review log is hash-chained and verifiable, but is not non-repudiation.
  • Runs and drafts can be deleted from the Runs page.
