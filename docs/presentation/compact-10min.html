Code-level refactor · drafts, not deployables

The lab shows the target shape. It does not compile it.

Evidence & audit · the trust model

A claim is only as strong as its locator, its hash, and its open question.

PreFlight does not store “a finding.” It stores a claim with a source pointer, an excerpt hash, a method, a confidence label, and — where evidence runs out — an assumption with an owner. The packet and audit live in two separate chains.
